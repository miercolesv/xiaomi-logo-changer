Ready-made boot logos for the **Xiaomi Mi 10 (umi)**: the boot logo, fastboot screen and error screen, in seven designs. Every picture is redrawn; no Xiaomi artwork is included.

- `mi10-<design>-fastboot.zip`: unzip, then `fastboot flash logo logo.img` (unlocked bootloader).
- `mi10-<design>-magisk.zip`: install from the Magisk or KernelSU app. Removing the module puts the original back.

Mi 10 only. For other Xiaomi phones, build your own from the phone's `logo.img`; see the README.
